Because getting started should feel simple.

Which subjects and age groups do you cover?＋

We offer support across school subjects from primary through Grade 12 / FSc, including maths, sciences, English, languages, humanities, computing, business, and economics. Tell us your exact subject and level to discuss availability.

Can I join from outside Pakistan?＋

Yes. Lessons are online, so students can join internationally. Share your country and time zone when enquiring so we can discuss a suitable schedule.

How do the online lessons work?＋

Lessons take place live online with a tutor. You’ll need a reliable internet connection, a computer or tablet, and your school materials. The lesson link and any other requirements are agreed before you start.

Can you help with exams and difficult topics?＋

Yes. Support can focus on understanding difficult concepts, practising questions, revising a course, or preparing for exams. Tutors guide learning; students complete their own assessed work.

What are the fees?＋

Fees depend on your subject, level, curriculum, and lesson arrangement. Share your requirements to request a suitable quote before committing to lessons.

Is the demo lesson free?＋

Yes. You can request a free introductory demo to discuss your needs and explore the lesson approach. Scheduling depends on subject and tutor availability.
